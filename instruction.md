Imagine several schools comparing two ways of studying for an exam. Each school reports its results.

Your job is to combine those results and explain what they tell us overall. This is called a meta-analysis.

Read the supplied studies and identify the relevant results. Combine the evidence, explain the overall finding, and describe the uncertainty. Show where the evidence came from and how it supports your conclusions.

Leave behind the evidence you used, your analysis, and a short explanation. Note any missing information or unfinished work.
