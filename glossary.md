# Shared glossary

Terms used in the calculation card and worked reference answer.

| Term | Explanation |
| --- | --- |
| Average score | The total of a group’s scores divided by the number of students. |
| Standard Deviation | Describes how spread out the students’ scores are within a group. |
| School’s estimated difference | The practice-question group’s average score minus the rereading group’s average score at that school. |
| Variance of the estimated difference | Measures the sampling uncertainty in a school’s estimated difference. This is different from the spread of individual students’ scores. |
| Weight | Determines how much a school contributes to the combined result. A larger weight gives the school more influence. |
| Weighted difference | A school’s estimated difference multiplied by its weight. |
| Total weight | The sum of the three schools’ weights. |
| Combined difference | The sum of the weighted differences divided by the total weight. |
| Standard error | Measures the sampling uncertainty in the combined difference. A smaller standard error means a more precise estimate. |
| Margin | The amount subtracted from and added to the combined difference to calculate the interval limits. |
| 95% confidence interval | A range expressing uncertainty in the combined difference under the stated assumptions. It is not the range of individual students’ scores. |
| Independent results | Results from different groups and schools are assumed not to depend on one another for these calculations. |
