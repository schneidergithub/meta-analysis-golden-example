# Shared glossary

Terms used in the calculation card and worked answer.

## Meta-analysis

Combining results from separate studies to find what they show overall.

## Average score

The total of a group's scores divided by the number of students.

## Sample

The students included in a study. In this exercise, each group is a sample of students.

## Standard Deviation

Describes how spread out the students' scores are within a group. A sample standard deviation is calculated from the scores of the students included in that group.

## School's estimated difference

The practice-question group's average score minus the rereading group's average score at that school.

## Variance of the estimated difference

A measure of how much a school's estimated difference could vary if the study were repeated with new groups of students. This is different from the spread of individual students' scores.

## Weight

Determines how much a school contributes to the combined result. A larger weight gives the school more influence.

## Weighted difference

A school's estimated difference multiplied by its weight.

## Total weight

The sum of the three schools' weights.

## Combined difference

The sum of the weighted differences divided by the total weight.

## Standard error

Estimates how much the combined difference would vary across repeated sets of studies. It describes uncertainty in the estimate, rather than differences among individual students.

## Margin

The amount subtracted from and added to the combined difference to calculate the interval limits.

## 95% confidence interval

A range expressing uncertainty in the combined difference under the stated assumptions. It is not the range of individual students' scores.

If similar studies were repeated many times, roughly 95% of the intervals calculated this way would include the actual difference, provided the calculation's assumptions hold.

## Independent results

Results from different groups and schools are assumed not to depend on one another for these calculations.
